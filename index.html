Apple OTA installation

Install a signed IPA

An IPA must already be signed and provisioned for the target device. This website does not create Apple certificates, bypass Apple's checks, or sign unsigned IPAs in Safari. Uploaded packages are accessible to anyone with their temporary link.

App's bundle identifier must match the package. No Apple ID, .p12 or private signing keys are requested.

Install on iPhone

After uploading, open the installation link on your iPhone in Safari. Apple may reject it if the IPA is not signed with suitable provisioning or is not eligible for over-the-air distribution.

OmnaLocation · Apple Maps

Choose a test location

See your chosen coordinates in Apple Maps and export a GPX point for Xcode testing. This does not alter system GPS or make other apps show you at the selected location.

How this works

Apple Maps opens your coordinates. GPX can be loaded into Xcode to simulate a location while debugging an app you develop. It doesn't override Find My, other native apps, or your IP address.
